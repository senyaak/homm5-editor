One whole model per piece (four walls in the donor's order; left, right and big tower; the gate; the moat), each stood where the donor's piece stands; the breached and razed models are optional and fall back to the one before. Tracks of your own — .ogg files over the set's
Each slot replaces that track of the set chosen above; a slot left blank keeps the set's. The files are copied under the game's Music folder on install — the game plays music from loose files, not from an archive.
