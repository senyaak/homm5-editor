Factions
A faction is a town type of our own: a shipped town copied and made ours, a race, named towns, a hero pool, a picker tile — and four numbers in the executable, set on install.

The AI — what a hero of the race thinks a skill is worth
Three numbers per skill, by the hero's role: a commander fights, a collector/supplier gathers, a freelancer roams. A row left blank is answered by the race chosen here; without one, a blank row is worth nothing. “Fill from” copies a race's whole column so it can be edited.

Buildings — the grid the build screen draws
One building per cell; its upgrades stack on it unless a level is given a cell of its own. A dependency is a cell above in the same column with nothing between (the arrow). Click a cell to edit it.
no cell chosen
Creatures — what the dwellings hire
Per tier: the base creature, its upgrade, and the second upgrade the upgraded dwelling hires beside it. A tier left as “the donor's” keeps hiring the donor's creatures; a tier of yours with no second upgrade hires none. A creature no dwelling hires has no town — the hire screen shows it as a neutral. The creatures are the mod's own (Units…).
Siege and exterior — whose parts
Siege parts of your own — models where the donor's pieces stand
One whole model per piece (four walls in the donor's order; left, right and big tower; the gate; the moat), each stood where the donor's piece stands; the breached and razed models are optional and fall back to the one before. A part with any model of yours is yours whole. The gate of yours does not animate.

Tracks and sounds of your own — over the set's
Tracks are .ogg, copied under the game's Music folder on install (the game plays music from loose files, not from an archive). Sounds — the town screen's ambient loop and the clicks of its buildings — are .wav (PCM) or .ogg, carried inside the mod. A slot left blank keeps the set's.
Pictures of your own — instead of the drawn or the donor's icons
PNG or GIF, fitted to the icon's size. A slot left blank is drawn from the theme when there is one, kept the donor's when there is not. A building's icon is set in its cell.
